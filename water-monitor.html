✦ problem

my fridge was leaking onto the floor — the kind of slow drip you don't notice until there's real damage. i wanted an early warning system instead of discovering it by stepping in a puddle. also became the basis for a broader research paper on how microcontroller-based solutions could help underserved communities detect and prevent water damage.

✦ thinking

✦ built

a microcontroller-based water leak detection system using arduino and water/moisture sensors. the system monitors for leaks and triggers an alert when water is detected. also the subject of an isp research paper exploring real-world applications of microcontrollers in underserved communities.
