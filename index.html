✦ about

grew up in peru obsessed with math. coming to the US as a junior in high school and ended up in UIUC with a passion in engineering.

microcontrollers are my thing. that moment when a physical sensor responds to something real — water dripping, a rep counted, a signal caught — nothing digital-only comes close. i think hardware first, then figure out the software that gives it meaning.

i care a lot about making things that actually work for real people. if it's annoying to use, or looks bad, or solves a fake problem — it's not done.
